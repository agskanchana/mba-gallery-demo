The free plugin

The free Medical Before After Gallery plugin on its own, with its limits of 12 cases and 4 categories. Pick a category, open a case and drag the slider to compare.

How to turn it on

Install the plugin, add your cases, and put [medbeafgallery] on a page. The tiles are Dashboard → Design → Category Navigation: Carousel.
