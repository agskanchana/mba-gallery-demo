Pro

Single case with progress stages

A case with a progress timeline shows its stages under the photo. Choose a stage to see it; a view button brings the comparison back.

How to turn it on

In a case: Progress timeline box, add a photo and date for each stage. The timeline shows when it has three or more photos; show_stages="false" hides it.
