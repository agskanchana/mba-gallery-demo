Pro

Someone like you

Visitors give their gender and age and say what they would like to improve. The people closest to them come first. Nothing they choose is stored or sent.

How to turn it on

Dashboard → Design → Gallery → How visitors find cases: Someone like you. For one gallery only: [medbeafgallery structure="match"]. It uses the gender and age on your cases.
